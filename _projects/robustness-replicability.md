As part of this many-researcher project, I reproduce a study published in the <em>Journal of Development Economics</em> using the original dataset. The project will provide replicability rates by collecting and analyzing replication reports of over 60 non-laboratory studies in economics.

[Project website](https://www.rwi-essen.de/en/projects/detail/robustness-and-replicability-in-economics-r2e-277)
