I am an applied economist working in environmental and development economics. My research studies how households, governments and international and non-governmental organisations in low- and middle-income countries respond to environmental shocks. I combine (quasi-)experimental research designs with household survey, administrative and spatial data.

Since 2023, I have been working as a postdoctoral researcher at the <a href='https://www.rwi-essen.de/en/rwi/team/person/lukas-mogge'>RWI – Leibniz Institute for Economic Research</a> (Berlin Office), where I am part of the research group on "Climate Impacts and Adaptation" and the Policy Lab on "Climate Change, Development and Migration". I am a a J-PAL Invited Researcher and an organizing member of the <a href='https://www.deveconberlin.org/'> Development Economics Network Berlin (DENeB) </a>.
